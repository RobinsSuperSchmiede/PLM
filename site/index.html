Good to know

First start takes a few minutes. The app sets itself up once: it uses the Python, Node.js and PostgreSQL already on your computer, or downloads them into its own folder (checked against pinned checksums). An internet connection is needed for that first start.

The installers are not code-signed yet. On Windows, SmartScreen may say it protected your PC: choose More info → Run anyway. On macOS, right-click the app and choose Open the first time. On Linux, make the AppImage executable (chmod +x).

Your data stays local. Everything lives in a database on your own computer; updates replace the app, never your data.
